What ExM Explorer shows

Within a decade of its invention, expansion microscopy has spread from a handful of animal cell lines across much of the eukaryotic tree. Yet the record of which organisms were expanded, with which protocol and with which antibody or dye, remains scattered over several hundred papers. ExM Explorer assembles that record into a single curated database, searchable by species, antibody, dye and study in the tabs, with its construction described under Methods.

1Literature search

PubMed and Europe PMC, which indexes the bioRxiv and medRxiv preprint servers, were queried on 27–28 April 2026 for expansion microscopy method names (expansion microscopy, U-ExM, ultrastructure expansion, proExM, ExPath, ExFISH, Magnify, cryo-ExM and others) published from 2015 onward. Records were deduplicated by DOI, giving 861 records.

2Full-text retrieval

Full texts were retrieved for records on both sides of the keyword screen (step 3): open-access PDFs by script (164), bioRxiv preprints from the bioRxiv server (224), and the remainder manually through institutional subscriptions. Papers from our laboratories and collaborators not returned by the search were added manually, giving 579 PDFs. PDFs are not redistributed; every record links to its source by DOI.

3Keyword screen

Titles and abstracts were scored for ExM, antibody and experimental terms (400 records retained, 461 rejected). As ExM is often used without being named in the abstract, the screen was not used to exclude papers, and relevance was assessed on the full text (step 4). Both assessments are recorded for every PDF.

4Extraction

Each PDF was converted to text (PyMuPDF 1.28.2) and read in full, one paper at a time and without cross-paper context, by a large language model (Claude Sonnet 4.6, claude-sonnet-4-6; Anthropic API). The model first determined whether the paper reported ExM experiments and then returned one record per species × label × paper, comprising the organism, sample type, fixation, anchoring, gel composition, homogenization, nominal and measured expansion factors, labeling strategy, and for each antibody, dye or stain its target, host, supplier, catalog number, dilution, fluorophore and outcome. Unreported fields were left empty, and inferred or uncertain values were prefixed inferred: or uncertain:.

Of the 579 PDFs, 525 yielded data, 51 were classified as not reporting ExM experiments, and 3 failed or contained no data. Species reported only in Supplementary Table S1 of Mikus et al. (2025) were added from that table.

5Curation

ExM variant names were standardized, species synonyms merged, and each species assigned to one of 15 clades. Preprints and their published versions were identified by title similarity and by shared authors and species, classified with the CrossRef API and confirmed with Claude Sonnet 4.6; 116 duplicate PDFs were removed, retaining the published version, and 30 further preprint–publication pairs with changed titles were merged under the published DOI. Two review articles were removed, and environmental ciliates missed by extraction were added manually. All changes are logged.

6Second extraction

To capture additional protocol fields and to assess reproducibility, all papers were extracted a second time with an expanded schema (Claude Sonnet 4.6). Three papers that failed this extraction retained their first-pass records. The database comprises {{STUDIES}} studies and {{ROWS}} rows.

7Normalization and taxonomy

Antibody, dye and supplier names were mapped to canonical forms, and antibodies listed as "alpha/beta-tubulin" were split by target. Missing suppliers and catalog numbers were recovered from the source text with Claude Haiku 4.5 (claude-haiku-4-5), which filled empty fields only. Antibodies obtained as gifts are recorded as "gift: [lab]" without a catalog number.

For counts and figures, cell lines, strains and tissues were resolved to their source organism (for example, HeLa and HEK293 to Homo sapiens), giving {{SPECIES}} species, and eukaryotes were placed on the UniEuk taxonomy as used in EukProt. To express coverage relative to sequenced diversity (Overview, Figure 1), the species of EukProt v3 were assigned to the same lineages by their UniEuk taxonomy and matched to expanded species by binomial name; expanded species without a EukProt counterpart are counted separately.

8Quality control

Eight controls were computed on the released database: core fields ≥96% filled; 90.5% of primary antibodies with a catalog number, clone, gift laboratory or supplier; agreement between the two extractions of 98.8% (clade), 99.4% (protocol outcome) and 95.7% (label outcome); a single clade per species across studies (100%) and a single target for 78.1% of antibodies used in two or more studies; 100% conformance to controlled vocabularies; valid syntax for 99.1% of catalog numbers; clade agreement with NCBI Taxonomy of 99.6%; and 291 values flagged uncertain: and 1,632 inferred:.

9Portal

This portal is a single HTML file generated from the database by a deterministic script, with all data embedded and no external resources loaded. Within each species, protocol records sharing every reported parameter were grouped, and each record lists the papers that reported it. In the Overview, the difficulty of each clade is the share of its species for which any study reported a partial or failed expansion, shown for clades with at least five species. Every number displayed was checked against the data, and all views, links and exports were tested.

10Data and code availability

The database, portal, code, pipeline scripts with their logs, and quality-control outputs are openly available. Code is released under the MIT License and data under CC BY 4.0.

!Limitations

  • Entries reflect what was published and are biased toward animals and model organisms; absence indicates that ExM has not been tried or reported, not that it fails.
  • Failed experiments are rarely published, so outcomes overestimate success.
  • Values were extracted automatically and may contain errors or omissions; species shown only in figures or supplements may be missing.
  • Antibody targets are those reported by the authors and were not validated.
  • Exact protocols should be taken from the original publications, linked by DOI.
